💼 Projects Overview

📈 1. Power BI: Sales Performance Dashboard

Objective:  
Analyze regional and product-based sales data to uncover trends, performance metrics, and business opportunities.

Features:
- Dynamic KPIs for revenue, profit margin, and transactions
- Slicer filters by region, category, and time
- Monthly sales and YOY comparison
- Highlighted underperforming areas for improvement.

---

📊 2. Tableau: Customer Demographics & Retention

Objective:
Explore customer demographics and behavioral patterns to optimize marketing and improve customer retention.

Features:
- Age group vs product preference heatmaps
- Gender-based purchasing behavior analysis
- Customer retention trends over time
- Regional activity using interactive maps
